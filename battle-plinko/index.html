Battle Plinko™

Drop the piece.
The fight follows.

A night stone pit. You and a myth. The ball picks the blow.

Battle Plinko is a portrait phone game from JosspaTech (pronounced Joss-pah-tech). The fighters stand on the cave floor. The ramp under them is iron pegs and buckets. You choose the gate. They do the rest.

What you do

  • Pick a gate — hold your side of the rail. Your fighter tosses a brass piece onto the ramp. It rolls. You do not see the path ahead of time.
  • The bucket is the blow — a sword, fire, ice, lightning, or a shield. The fighter on the floor actually swings, breathes, or brings the shield across.
  • Both sides drop — the creature throws on its own. A shield stops the next blow. A piece through the crack in the middle turns their bucket against them.
  • The next one is harder — beat the minotaur, and a serpent, chimera, cyclops, griffin, hydra, manticore, or Medusa is waiting. They do not fight the same way.

The room

Torchlight on stone. Worn brass on iron pegs. The piece stays bright enough to follow. The fighters stay large enough to read. Still between blows. They move when they attack, and when they are hit.

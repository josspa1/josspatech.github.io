FAQ

How the screens work is in the how-to videos above. These are the plan and policy questions people ask first.

How much does it cost?

15-day Premium trial, then $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr (same features; annual saves about two months). Billed through Apple or Google — not a JosspaTech account.

What’s the difference between monthly and annual?

Same Premium either way. Monthly is $9.99/mo. Annual is $74.99/yr. Pick one in the store when you subscribe.

How does the free trial work — and why a card?

Full Premium for 15 days. Apple and Google require a payment method to run a store trial; you are not charged during those days. Cancel in store subscriptions before it ends and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

App Store or Google Play → Subscriptions. Access continues until the period you already paid for ends. There is no JosspaTech login to hunt down.

Is my data private? Do you sell it?

Data stays on your phone. No ads. We don’t sell your financial data. Money comes from subscriptions, not your statement.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export CSV or JSON anytime. Optional encrypted cloud backup uses a key only you know. Your data is never locked in.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. PocketBudJet never asks for a bank login. Import a statement or type transactions — see the import how-to above for the steps.

Where is it available?

Official Google Play and App Store listings · US. How-to videos in eight languages.

How do I get help?

Start with the how-to videos above. Questions or feedback: support@josspatech.com — every message is reviewed; we may not reply to everyone.

What you can do

  • Bring in a statement — share a PDF or spreadsheet from the app that has it. The first screen shows the steps.
  • See the list — each subscription, what it costs, when it renews, and how steady the charges have been.
  • Get a reminder — the day before a renewal, a free trial ends, or the last day to cancel.
  • Cancel — opens that company's current cancel page. Some pages have a button. Some only tell you to call, visit, or cancel with Apple or Google. If Apple or Google bills you, Unscribe opens that store's subscription list.

Pro

The first five subscriptions stay free. Pro is $29.99 once, or $9.99 a year. No monthly plan. No free trial on the paid plan. Pro is the full list, reminders, price-increase and duplicate flags, cancel help for every subscription, and a CSV export.

FAQ

Plan and policy below. How-to chapters: watch the real app.

How much does it cost?

First five subscriptions free. Pro is $29.99 once or $9.99/yr — no monthly plan. Billed through Apple or Google.

What’s free vs Pro?

Free: five subscriptions. Pro: full list, reminders, price-increase and duplicate flags, cancel help for every subscription, and CSV export.

How do I cancel Pro?

Yearly: App Store or Google Play → Subscriptions. Lifetime is one-time — nothing renews. Access continues until a yearly period ends.

Is my data private? Do you sell it?

On your phone. No ads. We don’t sell your subscription list.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Pro includes CSV export so your list is never locked in. Watch: Export.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Share a PDF or spreadsheet from the app that already has the statement. Unscribe never asks for a bank login. Watch: Import a statement.

How do I import a statement?

Share a PDF or spreadsheet from the app that has it — no bank login. Watch: Import a statement.

How do I get reminders?

Settings → Remind me. Pick days before renewals and the time. Pro unlocks reminders. Watch: Reminders.

How do I cancel a subscription company?

Open that subscription’s detail for cancel help. Watch: Cancel help.

Where is it available?

Official Google Play and App Store listings · US.

If you searched for a YNAB alternative, you probably want the planning habits without handing a bank connection to another company — or without paying a premium subscription forever. This page compares that trade honestly and points to PocketBudJet when private statement import is the job.

What people usually want when they leave YNAB

YNAB teaches you to give every dollar a job. That method works. The friction people complain about is rarely the idea — it is the ongoing price, the feel of linking accounts, or wanting a simpler “import the statement I already have” path.

A useful alternative keeps the useful parts: budgets, bills, categories, and a clear picture of spending. It should not pretend to be a bank or require you to trust a permanent feed if you would rather share a file you downloaded yourself.

Neither app is “better” for everyone. YNAB wins if you want its method and are fine with its account model. PocketBudJet wins if private import, subscription spotting from statements, and keeping files on your device matter more.

What PocketBudJet actually does

  • Import bank and card statements you already can download.
  • Plan spending with budgets and bills; track debt.
  • Surface subscriptions and other recurring charges from those statements — renewals and what you might cancel — without a bank login.
  • Coach and weekly recaps on the phone; no ads.

It is not an open-source desktop ledger like GnuCash. It is a mobile budgeting app built for people who already have statement files and want clarity without another bank connection.

When to stay with YNAB

Stay if the envelope workflow is non-negotiable, your household already runs on YNAB's shared cloud habits, or you rely on features PocketBudJet does not claim. Switching apps mid-year for a small price difference alone is rarely worth the rebuild.

When to try PocketBudJet

Try it if you want statement-first import, you care that statements stay on the phone, you want recurring charges called out from the same files, and you prefer a lower annual Premium price with a short trial. Download from the official stores linked on the app page — never from a random APK site.

FAQ

Is PocketBudJet a free YNAB alternative?

There is a free tier with limits and a 14-day Premium trial. Ongoing Premium is paid. Treat “free forever full YNAB clone” claims elsewhere with skepticism.

Do I need to link my bank?

No. PocketBudJet is built around importing statements you export yourself.

Can it find subscriptions like a cancel helper?

It finds recurring charges from imported statements — what you pay and when it renews — so you can decide what to cancel. It is not a bank-login unsubscribe network.

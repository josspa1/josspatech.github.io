Chrono24 Alternative for Watch ID: Identify From a Photo

2026-11-10 · Handy Horology Helper

Key takeaways

  • Chrono24 is a marketplace — listings, asking prices, buying and selling. It is not built as a bench-side identifier.
  • If you need a Chrono24 alternative for what is this watch?, you want photo/clue identification, not another storefront.
  • Handy Horology Helper (HHH) is a pocket reference for shops, estate sales, auctions, and the bench: identify from photos and the clues you already have.
  • Use Chrono24 when you are ready to shop or sell; use HHH when you need a name, era, metal, or worth cue first.

People search “Chrono24 alternative” for two different jobs. One is shopping. The other is standing in front of a piece and needing to know what it is. This page is about the second job.

Marketplace vs identification

Chrono24 aggregates listings so you can compare dealers and prices. That is valuable when you already know the reference you want. It is the wrong tool when the dial is unfamiliar, the caseback is worn, or you only have a photo from an estate sale.

Identification tools answer: maker, model family, roughly when, what metal, and what a fair ballpark looks like — with honest confidence, not a fake certificate.

What HHH is for

  • Watches and clocks in front of you — shop, estate sale, auction, or bench.
  • Photo identify plus the clues you already have.
  • Privacy-focused use on your device; Premium features for deeper tools.

It is not a dealer network, not a payment escrow, and not a substitute for a professional appraisal when money or authenticity is on the line for a high-value sale.

When Chrono24 is still the right tab

Use Chrono24 (or similar marketplaces) when you know the reference, you want live listings, or you are actively buying or selling. Do not expect a marketplace to replace hands-on identification from a photo of an unknown piece.

FAQ

Is Handy Horology Helper a Chrono24 clone?

No. Chrono24 is commerce. HHH is identification and reference.

Can HHH tell me exact market price?

It gives worth cues and context. Exact sold prices still come from recent comps and dealers — marketplaces help there after you know what you are looking at.

Does it work offline?

Core use is on your phone; some identify features need connectivity. Check the current app for what works offline on your build.

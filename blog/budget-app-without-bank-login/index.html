Key takeaways

  • You can run a full budget without linking a bank account — import statements you already download, or enter transactions by hand.
  • “No bank login” is not one product type: some apps are mobile statement-import tools, some are envelope apps, some are local desktop ledgers.
  • PocketBudJet is built for statement import on your phone (CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, XLSX, PDF), budgets, bills, debt, and spotting recurring charges — without a bank connection.
  • YNAB, Actual Budget, Goodbudget, and GnuCash still win for people who want their method first. Pick the method, then the privacy path.

Searchers for a budget app without bank login or budget app no bank connection usually want privacy, fewer third-party feeds, or a bank that will not sync. This page compares that path honestly — including PocketBudJet, which JosspaTech builds.

What “without bank login” actually means

It means the app does not require Plaid-style bank linking to be useful. You download a statement from your bank or card issuer (or share a file), then import it — or you type transactions yourself.

It does not mean every competitor forbids bank sync. Some (YNAB, Monarch) offer sync as an option. The question for this page is: can you budget well if you refuse the login?

How statement import works (any of these apps)

  1. In your bank or card app / website, download a statement or transaction export (CSV or OFX are most common).
  2. Share or open the file in the budget app — or email it to yourself and pick it on the phone.
  3. Categorize once; later imports get faster.
  4. Build budgets, bills, and (where supported) subscription / recurring lists from those same rows.

You never hand the app your bank username. You hand it a file you already control.

FAQ

Can I budget without linking my bank account?

Yes. Import statements or enter transactions manually. PocketBudJet, Goodbudget, Actual Budget, GnuCash, and YNAB (manual path) all support working without a live bank connection.

What is the best budget app without bank login?

Best for statement-first mobile privacy: PocketBudJet. Best for classic envelopes: YNAB or Goodbudget. Best local-first DIY: Actual Budget. Best free desktop books: GnuCash.

Is PocketBudJet free?

There is no permanent free tier. You get 14 free days of Premium on your phone (no card required); then Premium is paid — see the store listing for current prices.

Will my bank block statement downloads?

Most banks still offer CSV or PDF statements in online banking. If a bank removes exports, manual entry or another bank’s export remains the fallback — that is a bank policy issue, not unique to one app.

Is this the same as a Mint alternative?

Mint was sync-and-dashboard. A no-login budget app is closer to “import what you already have.” If you want that path on a phone, start with PocketBudJet; if you want YNAB’s method, start with YNAB.

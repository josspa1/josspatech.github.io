PocketBudJet FAQ

Quick answers on trial, import without a bank login, Basic vs Pro, and privacy. For step-by-step help, see our help center.

What does the 15-day trial include?

The Pro toolkit: receipt scan, debt planner, AI coach, reports, and more. Import and manual entry stay free. The 15-day trial starts through Apple or Google — payment method required; cancel anytime before the trial ends. After 15 days, subscribe at $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr, or keep Basic. No bank login required.

Can I import bank data without connecting my bank?

Yes — and it works worldwide. Download a statement or export a CSV from your bank, or use Share → PocketBudJet from your bank app. See the Import chapter in the user manual. No bank login required.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. PocketBudJet is privacy-first. Import a statement or type transactions. Bank connect is not offered right now. See the Import chapter.

What is Basic vs Pro?

Basic is free: import, manual entry, add any accounts you want, dashboard, categories, goals, lock, dark mode, basic export. Pro adds the debt engine, Coach, reports, receipt scan, and the rest of the toolkit. Try Pro for 15 days, then $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your device in PocketBudJet’s private storage. We never store bank usernames or passwords. Optional cloud backup uses AES-256 encryption with a key only you know. Read Privacy Policy and Why Local First.

What’s the fastest way to import a PDF statement?

Open your bank app, find the statement, tap Share, pick PocketBudJet — six taps total. Parsed and ready to confirm in Import. See the Import chapter in the user manual.

Is there a permanent free tier?

Yes. After your 15-day Premium trial, import and manual budgeting stay free. Subscribe at $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr to keep Pro features like Coach, debt tools, and reports. No ads, no data sales.

Which languages does PocketBudJet support?

The app UI supports English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Italian, and Hindi (scaffold). Full narrated user manuals on this site: EN, ES, DE, ZH, FR, PT, IT, and HI. Topic walkthroughs are English-only today.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel anytime; Premium stays active until the period ends.

Still stuck?

Email support@josspatech.com (usually within 24 hours) or browse all walkthroughs. Beta feedback: feedback form.

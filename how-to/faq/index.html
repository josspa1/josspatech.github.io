PocketBudJet FAQ

Quick answers on trial, import, bank sync, Premium pricing, and privacy. For step-by-step help, see our walkthrough videos.

What does the 21-day trial include?

Full Premium features except automatic bank sync. You get import (CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, XLSX, PDF), receipt scan, budgets, debt planner, AI coach, exports, and more — no credit card required. After 21 days, subscribe at $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr to keep access.

Can I import bank data without connecting my bank?

Yes — and it works worldwide. Download a statement or export a CSV from your bank, or use the Share to PocketBudJet trick from your bank app. See Import Bank Data for the full walkthrough. No bank login required.

Is bank sync available in Europe or outside the US?

No. Automatic bank sync (Teller) supports US institutions only. EU and international users should use import — same powerful categorization, no ongoing connection fees. We market bank sync honestly as US-only; import is the global path.

When does bank sync unlock, and what does it cost?

Bank sync requires a paid Premium subscription — it is not included in the 21-day trial. Premium includes two linked banks; each additional bank is $3.99/month. If your subscription lapses, bank connections disconnect automatically. Details: Connect Bank walkthrough and How We Make Money.

Why isn’t bank sync free like some competitors?

Teller charges per connected account. We pass through those costs transparently instead of selling your data or showing ads. Import stays free on trial and Premium — you choose sync only if the convenience is worth it. That keeps reviews fair: you know before you subscribe.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your device in PocketBudJet’s private storage. We never store bank usernames or passwords. Optional cloud backup uses AES-256 encryption with a key only you know. Read Privacy Policy and Why Local First.

What’s the fastest way to import a PDF statement?

Open your bank app, find the statement, tap Share, pick PocketBudJet — six taps total. Parsed and ready to confirm in Import. Walkthrough: Share Bank Statements.

Is there a permanent free tier?

No. PocketBudJet is trial-first: 21 days of Premium (minus bank sync), then subscribe or lose access. No ads, no data sales — your subscription funds development.

Which languages does PocketBudJet support?

The app UI supports English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Italian, and Hindi (scaffold). Full narrated user guides on this site: EN, ES, DE, ZH, FR, PT, and IT. Topic walkthroughs are English-only today.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple App Store or Google Play — same as any app. Cancel anytime; Premium stays active until the period ends. Bank sync disconnects if you do not renew.

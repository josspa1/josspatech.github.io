PocketBudJet FAQ

Quick answers on trial, import without a bank login, Premium, and privacy. For step-by-step help, see the how to use guide.

What does the 14-day trial include?

The Premium toolkit: receipt scan, debt planner, Coach, reports, and more. You get 14 free days on your phone — no card required. Reminder on day 13; Premium locks on day 14 until you subscribe at $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr. No bank login required.

Do I need a card for the free days?

No. The 14 free days start on your phone with no card. A card is only needed when you subscribe through Apple or Google after the free days if you want to keep Premium. Cancel anytime in App Store or Google Play subscription settings. JosspaTech has no account that can charge you separately.

Can I import bank data without connecting my bank?

Yes — and it works worldwide. Download a statement or export a CSV from your bank, or use Share → PocketBudJet from your bank app. No bank login required. Watch: Import a statement.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. PocketBudJet is privacy-first. Import a statement or type transactions. Bank connect is not offered right now. Watch: Import a statement.

What is the 14-day trial vs Premium?

The trial is the full Premium toolkit for 14 days on your phone — no card required. Then $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr to keep it — debt engine, Coach, reports, receipt scan, and the rest. Subscribe through Apple or Google when you are ready. Cancel anytime.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your device in PocketBudJet’s private storage. We never store bank usernames or passwords. Optional backups live in your own Drive or iCloud — never on a JosspaTech server. Read Privacy Policy and Why Local First.

What if JosspaTech disappears?

Your data already lives on your phone. Export to CSV anytime. Optional backups live in your own Drive or iCloud — never on a JosspaTech server. If the app store listing ever goes away, you keep what is on the device and in your own backup.

What’s the fastest way to import a PDF statement?

Open your bank app, find the statement, tap Share, pick PocketBudJet — six taps total. Parsed and ready to confirm in Import. Watch: Import a statement.

Is there a permanent free tier?

No. PocketBudJet is 14 free days on your phone — no card required — then $9.99/mo or $74.99/yr when you subscribe. Cancel anytime. No ads, no data sales.

Which languages does PocketBudJet support?

The app UI supports English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Italian, and Hindi (scaffold). Full narrated user manuals on this site: EN, ES, DE, ZH, FR, PT, IT, and HI. Topic walkthroughs are English-only today.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel anytime; Premium stays active until the period ends.

FAQ

How Identify and the vault work is in the how-to videos above. These are the plan and policy questions.

How much does it cost?

15-day trial, then $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr. Same features either plan. Billed through Apple or Google.

What’s the difference between monthly and annual?

Same access either way. Monthly is $4.99/mo. Annual is $49.99/yr. Choose in the store when you subscribe.

How does the free trial work?

15 days through Apple or Google. Cancel in store subscriptions before it ends and you are not charged.

How do I cancel?

App Store or Google Play → Subscriptions. Access continues until the period you already paid for ends.

Is my data private? Do you sell it?

Nobody but you sees the vault. Records stay on your device. No ads. We don’t sell your collection data.

What categories are covered?

Coins, cards, stamps, paper money, and lighters.

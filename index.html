How PocketBudJet Works

Your finances
are your business.

Most finance apps make money from your data. PocketBudJet makes money from a fair price — and that's the whole business model. Your attention is not the product.

Your data lives on your device

Transactions, budgets, goals, and debts live in PocketBudJet’s private app storage on your phone. Turn on backups or cloud sync and you pick the provider — payloads are always AES-256 encrypted with a key only you know.

Bank connection is optional

Stay fully manual with imports and scans, or connect a bank with paid Premium via Teller when you want hands-off sync. Import as many months of statements as you want (CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, XLSX, PDF), compare spending month to month, and never require a bank login to get started.

No account required — ever

Open the app and start. No email, no password, no profile. When you delete the app, your data goes with it.

AI coach — on your device, on your terms

Ask the AI Financial Coach anything about your money in plain English. It uses only aggregated summaries — category totals, not individual transactions. Opt-in. Opt-out any time in Settings.

Your financial home base — and your translator

Bank export menus are meager. PocketBudJet imports anything — share-sheet straight from your bank app, CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF, XLSX, PDF — and exports clean data your accountant, financial planner, or mortgage broker can actually use. PDF, Excel, Word, OFX, HTML, and accountant-formatted reports. Hand off your year-end, refi packet, or planner upload in two taps. See how share-to-PocketBudJet works →

Why this matters in 2026

Most budget apps force bank login on day one. PocketBudJet starts with imports and manual entry, then offers optional Teller bank sync for paid Premium subscribers who want automation. You’re not locked into a middleman pipeline just to see where your money went.

Watches on the left, clocks on the right, shared tools underneath.

Watches
  • Barcode / UPC scanPoint at a retail box code to jump into Identify or add the piece to your collection — built for modern production watches, not just antiques.
  • Serial era datingEnter a serial and get an approximate production era band from public charts (Rolex, Omega, Seiko, and more) — honest confidence, not a factory database.
  • Identify historyReopen past IDs, photos, and confidence notes without re-shooting the piece at the flea or shop.
  • Photo CoachGuided multi-shot framing for dial, caseback, and details — listings, insurance, and cleaner Identify results.
  • Document scannerCapture papers, receipts, warranties, and certificates into the piece record.
  • Wear logA diary of what you actually wore — heatmap, streaks, and which pieces haven’t left the box lately.
  • Service trackerFull service and repair history with interval reminders so you don’t miss a service window.
  • Warranty trackerExpiry dates, claim notes, and deadline alerts for brand or dealer warranties.
  • Watch PassportA digital ID for each piece — share with a QR code, or write it to a blank NFC sticker you stick in the box or papers. Not the same as factory authenticity chips in some new watches.
  • Condition assessmentGrade dial, case, crystal, and movement over time for insurance and resale.
  • Rotation plannerPlan the week ahead — pick which watch is next so you’re not deciding from a cold drawer every morning.
  • Accuracy trackerLog how a piece gains or loses seconds so you know when it’s time for regulation or service.
  • Compare & trade analyzerSide-by-side specs and a fairness read before you swap or buy up.
  • Atomic clock & moon phaseNTP reference time and lunar phase helpers for setting complications accurately.
Clocks
  • Movement identifyRead case stamps and plates, then match the calibre so you know what you’re repairing or ordering.
  • Beat meter & timegrapherListen to rate and beat error at the bench — adjust before you chase parts.
  • Pendulum calculatorLength and rate math for pendulum clocks when the stick or bob needs a real number, not a guess.
  • Movement reference & partsBrowse calibres and crosswalk replacement parts so the order matches the stamp on the plate.
  • Strike helperCount and sync strike trains when the clock strikes the wrong hour or won’t settle.
  • Complication calculatorMoon, date, and set helpers for clocks with more than a single train.
  • AI troubleshootingTroubleshoot a stopped or erratic clock with guided diagnosis, then jump to matching parts from the stamp.
  • Gear teeth counterCount teeth from a photo or on-device estimate when catalogs list tooth counts, not just names.
  • Clock repair projectsTrack a repair job against a piece in your collection so notes, parts, and progress stay with the clock.

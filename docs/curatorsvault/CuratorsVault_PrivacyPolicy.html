Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026 Developer: JosspaTech Contact: support@josspatech.com
The short version: Your collection lives on your device by default. We don't ask for your name, address, or payment details. Optional AI identification sends photos or descriptions you choose to a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker, which calls Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude — never with your identity attached. Optional cloud sync uses PocketBase only if you enable it. You control outbound data in Settings → Privacy.

1. Information We Collect

What we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, address, phone number, or email (unless you enter them in your own collection notes)
  • Social Security numbers or government IDs
  • Bank account numbers, routing numbers, or card numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Device contacts or contact lists
  • Biometric data (Face ID / fingerprint are verified by your phone's OS — CVC never receives the biometric)

What you enter into the app: Collectible items, photos, notes, prices, wish lists, and similar collection data are stored in CVC's private app storage on your device.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

On your device. Your collection is kept in CVC's private app storage sandbox. Sensitive settings (PIN hash, biometric-lock flag, OAuth tokens if you create them) are stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore via expo-secure-store.

Optional cloud sync. If you enable PocketBase cloud sync, collection data you choose to sync is transmitted over TLS to josspatech.pockethost.io. This is off by default.

On the network. All traffic to services listed below is over TLS.

3. Camera and AI Identification

Camera access is used to photograph collectibles for identification and your collection gallery. Photos are stored locally unless you enable cloud sync.

AI identification (optional). When you use Identify or AI chat features, the photo or text description you submit is sent over TLS to a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker (cvc-worker). The Worker may forward content to:

  • Google Gemini API — for photo-based identification of coins, cards, stamps, currency, and lighters
  • Anthropic Claude API — for description-only identification, AI chat advisor, and structured extraction

The Worker authenticates your app with a shared key (X-CVC-Key) — not your name or account. Images and descriptions are processed in-flight; JosspaTech does not retain them after the structured response is returned. Google and Anthropic do not retain API inputs for training under their commercial API terms.

You can use CVC's core collection features without AI identification.

4. eBay and Marketplace Search

CVC may query eBay's public Browse API and affiliate links to show listings relevant to your search or wish list. Only search terms and item metadata you initiate are sent — not your full collection or identity.

5. Certification Lookups (PSA / PCGS)

When you verify a grading cert number, CVC sends only the cert number to PSA or PCGS public APIs over TLS. No other personal data is included.

6. Web Companion

The optional Web Companion runs a local web server on your home WiFi network so you can browse your collection from a PC browser. Data stays inside your LAN and is not sent to JosspaTech.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. JosspaTech does not collect, store, or process payment card information. Subscription status may be verified through RevenueCat using an anonymous app user ID — not your name or email.

8. Diagnostics

CVC may collect anonymous crash diagnostics (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version) to fix bugs. Crash reports do not contain your collection photos or item details unless you explicitly include them in a feedback report.

9. Children's Privacy

Curator's Vault: Classics is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact support@josspatech.com if you believe a child has provided information through the app.

10. Data Deletion

Because your collection is stored on your device, you delete it by uninstalling the app. If you enabled PocketBase sync, contact us to request account deletion. JosspaTech does not retain a copy of your on-device collection.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date when we do. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Developer: JosspaTech · support@josspatech.com

The short version: Unscribe reads your statements on your phone. Your statements and subscription list are not uploaded to JosspaTech. There is no account, no bank login, and no advertising. A few requests leave the phone, and each one is described below.

1. What Unscribe does not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Bank logins, passwords, account numbers, or card numbers
  • Your statements, transactions, or subscription list
  • Your location, contacts, or photos
  • An advertising identifier. Unscribe does not use Meta, Firebase, or any ad network, and it does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps.

2. Statements you import

When you import a statement (PDF, CSV, OFX, QFX, or QIF), Unscribe reads it on your phone to find repeating charges. The file is not uploaded to JosspaTech. Unscribe only receives the file you choose to open.

3. Where your data is stored

Your subscription list, settings, and the charges Unscribe found are kept in Unscribe's private storage on your phone. They are not backed up to JosspaTech. If you export a CSV, the file goes only where you send it.

4. Reminders

Renewal and free-trial reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. No reminder data is sent to a server.

5. Voice

If you say a subscription instead of typing it, Unscribe uses the microphone and the phone's on-device speech recognizer. The audio is not sent to JosspaTech.

6. Cancel helper

When you tap to cancel a subscription, Unscribe opens your store's subscriptions page, or a web search for that company's cancel page, in your browser. The service name is part of that search. Unscribe does not cancel anything for you and does not sign in to any service.

7. Purchases

Unscribe Pro is sold through the app store on your phone. JosspaTech never sees your payment details. Purchase status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and renewal events, and basic device information. It does not receive your name, your statements, or your subscription list. RevenueCat's policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

8. Exchange rates

If you track a price in a currency other than your home currency, Unscribe requests a public exchange-rate table from frankfurter.dev. The request asks for the rate table only. It does not include your statements, subscription names, or amounts. The saved rates stay on your phone and are refreshed no more than once a day.

9. Children

Unscribe is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Deleting your data

In Unscribe, go to Settings and choose Delete all my data. Uninstalling Unscribe also removes everything it stored on that phone. JosspaTech holds no copy of your subscriptions or statements.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change.

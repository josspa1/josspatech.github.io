Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026 Developer: JosspaTech Contact: support@josspatech.com
The short version: Your watch and clock collection lives on your device by default. We do not require an account to use the app. AI identification sends a photo (or text clues you type) to a JosspaTech-operated server only when you ask for an ID. Optional cloud sync, crowd contributions, and PocketBudJet purchase linking are all off by default and controlled in Settings → Privacy Control.

1. Information We Collect

What we do NOT collect automatically:

  • Your name, postal address, phone number, or email (unless you choose to enter contact details for your own dealer/service records)
  • Bank account numbers, routing numbers, or payment card numbers
  • Real-time bank or financial institution credentials
  • Precise GPS location (the app may use coarse region for event suggestions if you enable location features on your device)
  • Your device contacts list
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted ads — Handy Horology Helper shows no third-party ads

What you enter into the app: Collection items (photos, descriptions, purchase prices, service records, wish lists, dealer contacts, and similar collector data) are stored locally in the app’s private database on your phone or tablet.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

On your device. Your collection, photos, and settings are kept in HHH’s private app storage (SQLite). Sensitive tokens, if any, use the platform secure store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Optional encrypted backup. If you export or back up, you choose the destination. Backups can respect Privacy Control toggles that strip financial fields, contact links, or deal history before export.

Optional cloud sync. PocketBase sync is off by default. When enabled, only the fields you allow in Privacy Control are transmitted over TLS to JosspaTech’s PocketBase instance. You can disable sync at any time.

3. Camera and Photos

Camera and photo-library access are used to photograph watches and clocks for your collection, insurance reports, and AI identification. Photos remain on your device unless you explicitly request AI identification or enable cloud sync/export.

4. AI Identification and Chat

When you tap Identify (photo or text), the app sends over TLS to a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker:

  • A base64-encoded photo and/or the text description you provide
  • The category (watch vs. clock) needed to route the request
  • An app authentication header — not your name or email

The Worker may forward the request to Google Gemini for visual or text analysis. Google does not retain Gemini API inputs for model training under Google’s API terms. Images and descriptions are processed in-flight to return identification results; JosspaTech does not use them for advertising.

Free-tier users receive a limited number of AI identifications per month; identification continues to work after limits with reduced frequency — the app does not hard-block saving results you already received.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play via RevenueCat. We receive subscription status and anonymized purchase metadata needed to unlock Pro features — not your full payment card details.

6. eBay, Clockworks, and External Links

Market search, wish-list alerts, and Clockworks parts guidance open or query external services (eBay, Clockworks.com). Affiliate links may be used on eBay searches; purchases happen on those third-party sites under their policies. HHH does not receive your eBay or Clockworks login credentials.

7. Optional PocketBudJet Link

If you use PocketBudJet (a separate JosspaTech app), you may optionally link a purchase to a collection item. Linking requires action in both apps; no automatic sharing of your full PocketBudJet ledger occurs without your confirmation.

8. Crowd Contributions (Opt-In)

You may optionally contribute anonymized identification or market signals to improve results for all collectors. Contributions exclude direct identifiers (names, serial numbers you mark private, exact addresses). This is controlled in Settings → Privacy Control and is off by default.

9. Local Device Sharing (Bluetooth)

Swap-meet sharing uses local Bluetooth between nearby devices with a PIN you choose. Data is not routed through JosspaTech servers for this feature.

10. Notifications

Push notifications (e.g., wish-list price alerts) require permission on your device. Alert rules and item references are stored locally; delivery uses Apple/Google push infrastructure.

11. Children’s Privacy

Handy Horology Helper is designed for adult collectors and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Privacy Control includes options to wipe financial fields, contacts/deals, or all local data. For cloud-synced data, disable sync and contact support@josspatech.com to request server-side deletion.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@josspatech.com

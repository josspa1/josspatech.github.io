How to delete your data

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 · Contact: support@josspatech.com
Short version: Your collection lives on your device by default. Erase it in Settings, or uninstall the app. Optional cloud sync / Crowd ID contributions can be turned off and removed separately.

Option 1 — Erase inside the app

  1. Open Handy Horology Helper
  2. Go to Settings (Profile)
  3. Open Backup / data management
  4. Use the controls to clear local collection data, or restore from a backup after intentional wipe
  5. Confirm any destructive prompts

That removes local watches, clocks, photos stored in the app database, wish list, and related on-device records. The app returns toward a first-run state.

Option 2 — Uninstall

Uninstalling Handy Horology Helper deletes local app storage. There is no required JosspaTech cloud account that retains your museum after uninstall unless you previously enabled optional sync.

Optional cloud sync / crowd contributions

PocketBase sync and anonymized Crowd ID write-back are off by default (Settings → Privacy Control). If you enabled them:

  • Turn sync / contribution toggles off in Privacy Control
  • Email support@josspatech.com from the address you used for any optional account or sync identity and request deletion of server-side copies

Purchases

Google Play / Apple subscription records are managed by the stores. Cancel or request refunds through Play Store / App Store account settings. JosspaTech does not store payment card numbers.

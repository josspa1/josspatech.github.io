Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026 Developer: JosspaTech Contact: support@josspatech.com
The short version: Pocket Allowance Ledger (PAL) is local-first. Family and kid program data is stored on the parent’s phone. The kid companion runs only on your home Wi‑Fi. JosspaTech does not operate a cloud backend for PAL v1 and does not sell your data.

1. Who PAL is for

The primary user is a parent or guardian. Children ages 6+ may use a parent-controlled browser dashboard on the same home network. PAL is not a kid banking, debit-card, or custodial-account product.

2. Information stored on your device

PAL stores family program data locally (typically SQLite on the parent phone), including items such as:

  • Child display names and PINs you choose
  • Contracts, tokens, rewards, and related household settings
  • Optional notes you enter in the app

This information stays on your device unless you export or back it up yourself.

3. Kid companion on your local network

When you enable the kid web companion, the parent app may serve a simple dashboard on your home LAN (for example via pal.local). That traffic stays on your network. PAL requests local-network permission only to provide this feature — not for GPS tracking or advertising.

4. What JosspaTech does not collect (v1)

  • No JosspaTech cloud account is required for core features
  • No bank credentials, debit cards, or custodial balances
  • No sale of personal or behavioral data
  • No cross-app tracking for advertising

5. Optional backups you control

If you use optional backup to iCloud or Google Drive, the export goes to your cloud account. JosspaTech does not host that backup.

6. Purchases

If subscriptions or in-app purchases are offered, billing is handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive your full payment card details.

7. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Parents control the household setup. Children interact through a parent-managed local experience. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information on JosspaTech servers in v1.

8. Data deletion

You can delete family data in the app (Settings) and/or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local app storage on that device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

10. Contact

Questions: support@josspatech.com

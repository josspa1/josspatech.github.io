Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Developer: JosspaTech · support@josspatech.com

The short version: Snip reads your statements on your phone. Your statements, charges, and subscription list never leave your phone, and JosspaTech never receives them. There is no account and no bank login. Snip uses a small number of outside services only for purchases and to measure our ads, and none of them receive your financial data.

1. What Snip does not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Bank logins, passwords, account numbers, or card numbers
  • Your statements, transactions, or subscription list
  • Your location, contacts, or photos

2. Statements you import

When you import a statement (PDF, CSV, OFX, QFX, or QIF), whether you pick it in Snip or share or open it from another app, Snip reads it on your phone to find repeating charges. The file is not uploaded anywhere. Snip only receives the one file you choose to share with it.

3. Where your data is stored

Your subscription list, settings, and the charges Snip found are kept in Snip's private storage on your phone. They are not backed up to JosspaTech. If you export a CSV, the file goes only where you send it.

4. Reminders

Renewal and free-trial reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. No reminder data is sent to a server.

5. Cancel helper

When you tap to cancel a subscription, Snip opens your App Store or Google Play subscriptions page, or a web search for that company's cancel page in your browser. Only the service name is part of that search. Snip does not cancel anything for you and does not sign in to any service.

6. Purchases

Snip Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. JosspaTech never sees your payment details. Purchase status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and renewal events, and basic device information. It does not receive your name or your financial data.

7. Ad measurement

To tell whether our ads bring people to Snip, the app may send events such as app installed or opened, a statement imported (not its contents), the subscription screen viewed, and purchases and renewals with the plan and price. These events carry an app or device identifier, app version, and basic device information. They never include your statements, charges, amounts you track, or the names of your subscriptions. Processors are Meta (privacy policy), Google Firebase (privacy policy), and RevenueCat.

On iPhone, Snip asks Apple's "Allow tracking" question first. If you do not tap Allow, your advertising ID is not sent. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

8. Children

Snip is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Deleting your data

In Snip, go to Settings → Delete all my data. Uninstalling Snip also removes everything it stored. JosspaTech holds no copy of your subscriptions or statements, so there is nothing to delete on our end.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.

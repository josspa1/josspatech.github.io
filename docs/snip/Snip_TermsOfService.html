1. What Snip is

Snip helps you find and keep track of subscriptions. It reads statements you import on your phone, lists repeating charges, totals what they cost, reminds you before renewals, and helps you find where to cancel. Snip does not connect to your bank, does not move money, and does not cancel subscriptions for you. You cancel with the company or store yourself.

2. Accuracy

Snip finds subscriptions by looking for repeating charges. It can miss a subscription, include a charge that is not one, or estimate a date or price that turns out to be wrong. Check important dates and amounts with the company or your statement. Snip is not financial advice.

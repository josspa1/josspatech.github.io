1. Getting Started

User asks

How do I install PocketBudJet and finish first-run setup?

What you'll see

Splash, feature showcase, Terms and Disclaimer tabs, optional app lock, then Home. A short wayfinding tour highlights the tab bar, header, drawer, Toolbox, and quick-add button. A 9-step setup wizard is available from Settings when you want guided budget setup.

What to do

  1. Install from Google Play (iOS coming soon), then open PocketBudJet and wait through the splash.
  2. Swipe the feature showcase and tap Get Started.
  3. On first launch, scroll Terms of Service and Disclaimer to the bottom, then tap I Accept — Continue.
  4. On Secure Your Data, turn on app lock if you want PIN or biometrics, then continue to Home.
  5. Complete the wayfinding landmarks (tab bar, search/Coach header, drawer, Toolbox, quick-add).
  6. Optional: open Settings → setup wizard. Enter name, currency, accounts, gross and take-home pay, bills, goals, budgeting style, and accessibility preferences.
  7. Want paycheck deductions itemized later? Tap any income transaction and choose Paycheck Breakdown.
  8. Enjoy a 15-day free trial with full app access — no credit card required.

3. Adding Transactions

User asks

How do I log spending or income by hand?

What you'll see

Quick-add from any screen, or Activity with Scan / Import / Add. Manual entry covers amount, merchant, category, optional split, notes, and attachments.

What to do

  1. Open Activity, or tap the gold plus from any screen.
  2. Choose Enter Manually (or Scan / Voice when that fits).
  3. Enter the amount first while it is fresh.
  4. Type the merchant the same way each time, then pick a category.
  5. For multi-category receipts, tap Split and assign portions.
  6. Tap Save — budget, account balance, and dashboard update immediately.

4. Importing Data

User asks

How do I bring in bank history without giving PocketBudJet my bank login?

What you'll see

Import Center / Toolbox entry points, file pickers, review drafts, duplicate handling, and Import History of past files.

What to do

  1. Fastest path: in your bank app, open the statement and share it into PocketBudJet (no Downloads folder needed).
  2. Or open Toolbox → Import Center and pick CSV, OFX/QFX/QIF/XLSX, PDF statement, screenshot, or email receipt.
  3. Review the draft, resolve duplicates, then confirm the import.
  4. Open Import History anytime to re-open drafts or audit what changed.

Also available: notification capture from banking apps; multi-month imports for pattern comparison. No bank login or credentials required for file import.

27. Data & Storage Management

User asks

How do I keep storage under control without losing my financial history?

What you'll see

Backup retention, on-device working-set controls, receipt-image storage, annual summaries, free-space preview, and archive-history status under Settings → Data & Backup.

What to do

  1. Choose a backup-retention window that matches how much detailed history you need readily available.
  2. Set the on-device working set based on how much offline history you want on the phone.
  3. Tune receipt-image preview storage separately from full backup retention.
  4. Use Review & Free Up Space before removing anything, and confirm archive status before assuming older years are fully stored.

Backup Retention

  • Choose how far back your backup keeps full transaction details: 6 months up to 7 years
  • Data that ages out is summarized — annual totals, top categories, and net savings are locked permanently
  • Summaries are yours forever regardless of which retention window you choose

On-Device Working Set

  • Controls how many months of transactions live on your phone for instant offline access
  • Minimum 3 months — the floor PocketBudJet needs to reliably track budget patterns
  • Extend up to 2 years if you want more history available without fetching from backup

Receipt Image Storage

  • Three-tier model: thumbnails always stay on device, full images live in backup, compressed previews are tiered
  • Choose your preview window: 30, 60, 90, or 180 days on-device before moving to backup-only
  • Tap any receipt to fetch the full image from backup whenever you need it

Annual Summaries

  • Year-by-year totals computed and locked permanently before any data ages out
  • Income, expenses, and net savings for every year you've used the app — never deleted
  • Power your all-time analysis even after underlying transactions have been archived

Free Up Space

  • Tap 'Review & Free Up Space' to preview exactly what would be removed before anything happens
  • Preview shows transaction count, receipt image impact, and estimated space reclaimed
  • Annual summaries are always computed and backup-confirmed before any data leaves the device

Archive History

  • Track the status of every archived year: transaction count, backup confirmation, and completion date
  • Years show as 'Pending' until backup confirms receipt — data stays on device until confirmed
  • Full audit trail so you always know what has been safely stored

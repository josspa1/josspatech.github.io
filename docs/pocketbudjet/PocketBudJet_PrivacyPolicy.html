Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026 Developer: JosspaTech Contact: support@josspatech.com
The short version: We don't ask for your name, address, SSN, or bank credentials. Your financial data lives on your device by default — you decide if you want to back it up to a cloud provider you pick. A few transaction-level details are sent (anonymized) to power smart features like AI coaching, crowd benchmarks, merchant categorization, and suggested category splits — the sections below list exactly what, when, and why. Every outbound data flow that contributes to the shared crowd model can be turned off in Settings → Data & Privacy without affecting the rest of the app.

1. Information We Collect

What we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, address, phone number, or email address (unless you choose to enter them for your own records, or you opt in to Email Forwarding under §4 in which case your forwarding email address is stored to route inbound messages to your device)
  • Social Security numbers or government IDs
  • Bank account numbers, routing numbers, or card numbers
  • Passwords to any external services
  • Real-time bank or financial institution credentials
  • Precise location data
  • Device contacts or contact lists
  • Biometric data (Face ID / fingerprint are verified by your phone's OS — PocketBudJet never receives the biometric)
  • Persistent advertising identifiers tied to your financial data

What you enter into the app: Transactions, budgets, debts, goals, categories, and similar financial entries are stored in PocketBudJet's private app storage on your device. You can optionally enter a zip code and country to participate in crowd benchmarks (§10) — this is the only semi-identifying data we ask for, and it remains optional.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

On your device. Your data is kept in PocketBudJet's private app storage sandbox. On iOS, that storage is protected by Apple's Data Protection; on Android, by the platform's app-data isolation. Sensitive items like your PIN hash, biometric-lock flag, and any OAuth tokens you create are stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore via expo-secure-store.

If you turn on backups or sync. You pick the cloud provider (iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox). Before anything leaves your device, the payload is compressed and encrypted with AES-256-CTR + HMAC-SHA256 using a key derived from a password only you know. JosspaTech has no copy of that password and cannot decrypt your backups.

On the network. All traffic to the services listed in §4, §5, §10, and §11 is over TLS.

3. Camera and Receipt Scanning

Camera access is used for receipt scanning. By default, receipt images are processed with on-device OCR first (Apple Vision / Android ML Kit) — the image only leaves your phone if on-device OCR can't read it clearly, in which case a cloud fallback is used for better accuracy.

Cloud OCR fallback (on by default). When on-device OCR returns low confidence, the image is sent over TLS to OCR.Space for text extraction. OCR.Space does not retain uploads after processing. This is on by default and can be disabled in Settings → Advanced.

AI Vision fallback (rare). If both on-device OCR and OCR.Space fail to produce usable receipt data, the image may be forwarded — over TLS, through a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker — to Google's Gemini API for structured extraction (merchant, date, items, total). Google does not retain Gemini API inputs for training under its API terms. The image is processed in-flight; neither JosspaTech nor Google retains it after the structured response is returned. You can disable cloud-based parsing entirely (OCR.Space + Gemini) in Settings → Advanced.

3a. PDF Statement and Document Import

When you import a PDF (bank statement, credit-card statement, paystub, invoice, or receipt) — by sharing it from another app, browsing files, or forwarding via email — PocketBudJet uses a layered parsing approach. Each layer is tried in order; processing stops at the first one that succeeds.

  • Layer 1 — Local text scan. Many PDFs already contain extractable text. We first try to read that text directly on your device, with no network call.
  • Layer 2 — Worker text extraction. If the on-device scan fails, the PDF bytes are sent over TLS to a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker, which uses an open-source PDF text-extraction library (unpdf) to pull the raw text. The text is returned to your device for parsing. The PDF is processed in memory and is not retained on the Worker.
  • Layer 3 — AI Vision. If the previous layers can't make sense of the document (scanned/image-only PDFs, paystubs with complex layouts, multi-account statements), the PDF is forwarded — through the same Worker — to Google's Gemini API, which returns structured transactions or a paystub breakdown. Google does not retain Gemini API inputs for training under its API terms. The PDF is processed in-flight and is not retained by JosspaTech or Google after the structured response is returned.

What is sent: the PDF bytes themselves. Bank statements typically include the account holder name, masked account number, posting dates, and transaction descriptions. The full PDF is required for Layer 3 because the AI needs the visual layout to identify rows accurately. Nothing is added to the upload — no device identifiers, no JosspaTech account ID (PocketBudJet does not require an account).

What is NOT sent: any other data on your device, your other PDFs, your transactions database, or your settings.

Disabling: the cloud parsing layers can be disabled in Settings → Advanced. With them off, only Layer 1 (on-device) runs, which works for most modern bank PDFs but will fail on scanned or image-only documents.

4. Email Forwarding (Optional)

PocketBudJet can ingest financial emails (bank confirmations, billing statements, payment receipts) that you forward to a unique inbound address. This feature is off by default and only activates when you enable it in Settings → Import & Connections → Email Forwarding.

How it works:

  • You enter the email address you'll be forwarding from. That address is registered with a JosspaTech-operated Cloudflare Worker so inbound messages can be routed to your device.
  • You forward (or set up an auto-forward in your mail provider) financial emails to a unique PocketBudJet inbound address.
  • The forwarded message is received by Postmark (a service of ActiveCampaign, LLC) over TLS. Postmark performs DMARC verification and posts the parsed message to the JosspaTech Worker.
  • The Worker sends the email subject and body over TLS to Anthropic, Inc. (the maker of the Claude AI) to extract structured transaction data.
  • The structured result (date, amount, merchant, currency) is queued for your device. Your device picks it up, lets you review, and you decide whether to save it.

What is sent to processors:

  • Postmark: the forwarded message itself (sender, subject, body, attachments) plus delivery metadata. Postmark is used solely as an inbound mail receiver; messages that fail DMARC are rejected.
  • Anthropic: the email subject and body for parsing into structured fields. Anthropic does not retain customer API inputs for training (see Anthropic's Commercial Terms and privacy policy).

What is NOT sent: your name, phone number, postal address, account numbers, or any data unrelated to the forwarded email.

Few-shot learning corpus (added May 2026): When you confirm a forwarded transaction in PocketBudJet, the list of line items extracted from the message (e.g. "eggs, bread, Tide pods") plus the category you confirmed (e.g. "Groceries") is sent to the JosspaTech Worker and stored as an example for the merchant. The Worker uses the most recent 5 examples per merchant to prompt Anthropic on future extractions from the same merchant, improving accuracy over time. No amounts, dates, or personal identifiers are sent with these examples. You can opt out of contributing to this corpus in Settings → Data & Privacy → "Help PBJ learn from everyone." When opted out, your confirmations are still saved locally but nothing is sent to the Worker.

Retention:

  • Forwarded message bodies are processed in-flight and are not retained after structured extraction.
  • The mapping of {your forwarding address → your device} is stored on the JosspaTech Worker for as long as the feature stays enabled. Disabling Email Forwarding removes the mapping.
  • Parsed transactions waiting for your device to pick them up are kept for up to 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Few-shot examples (items + category per merchant) are retained on the Worker indefinitely as part of the shared learning corpus. They are not linked to your device or identity.

Disabling: Settings → Import & Connections → Email Forwarding → Disable. This removes your forwarding address mapping from the Worker; subsequent emails are discarded.

5. AI Financial Coach (Optional)

PocketBudJet's AI features come in two layers:

On-device AI (always on, free): Category prediction, anomaly detection, and spending forecasts run on your device. Nothing is transmitted for these.

AI Financial Coach (premium, opt-in): When you use a coach feature, a summary is sent over TLS to Anthropic, Inc. (the maker of the Claude AI) to generate personalized guidance. The payload includes:

  • Category spending breakdown (e.g. "Groceries: $380")
  • Top merchant names with aggregated totals (e.g. "Target: $210" — no dates, no individual transactions)
  • Monthly income, monthly spending, total debt, and calculated savings rate

What is NOT sent to Anthropic: Your name, email, phone, address, account numbers, card numbers, device identifiers, or individual transaction-level records. Anthropic does not retain customer API inputs for training (see Anthropic's Commercial Terms and privacy policy).

The AI Financial Coach is disabled by default and can be toggled in Settings. You can use PocketBudJet indefinitely without ever turning it on.

6. Bank Account Connectivity (Teller — paid Premium only)

PocketBudJet offers optional automatic bank sync for active paid Premium subscribers through Teller (Teller Financial, Inc.). It is not available during the 21-day Premium trial. If you do not use bank sync, your imports work the same as always: CSV / OFX / QFX / QIF / XLSX files, PDF statements, receipt scans, Share-to-PocketBudJet, and optional Email Forwarding (§4).

What you do: You connect your bank inside the app using Teller Connect. Your bank login credentials are entered directly with Teller — JosspaTech never sees or stores them.

What Teller sees: Your bank credentials (to authenticate), account identifiers, and transaction data your bank makes available through Teller's API.

What JosspaTech sees: An enrollment token relayed through our Cloudflare Worker so your device can fetch transactions. We do not receive your bank password. Transactions retrieved through Teller are stored locally on your device.

Disconnecting: You can disconnect any bank in Settings → Bank Connections. Disconnecting removes the stored token from your device. Previously imported transactions remain on your device.

End of subscription or trial: If your paid subscription lapses or your trial ends without subscribing, bank connections are automatically disconnected.

7. Voice Shortcuts

Voice shortcuts (Siri on iOS, Google Assistant / App Actions on Android) are opt-in and off by default. Voice audio is processed by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies — not by JosspaTech. Only the resulting text command is used to create a transaction locally on your device.

7a. Voice Input on the Coach Screen

The AI Coach screen offers an optional microphone button for hands-free questions. When you tap it, your speech is transcribed on-device by Apple's Speech framework (iOS) or the Android system speech recognizer (typically Google). The audio recording itself never leaves your phone. Only the resulting text — what you would have typed — is sent to the AI Coach. You can decline microphone permission and the rest of the Coach continues to work via typing.

7b. "Share to PocketBudJet" Receiver

PocketBudJet registers as a target in your phone's system Share sheet. When you choose to share a receipt photo, a bank-statement PDF, or text from another app, only that one item is passed to PocketBudJet for the duration needed to import it. We do not access your other apps' data, your photo library, or any other content unless you specifically share it with us.

8. Subscriptions and Payments

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. JosspaTech does not collect, store, or process payment card information. Subscription status is verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID — not your name or email.

9. Diagnostics and Crash Reports

PocketBudJet uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash diagnostics so we can find and fix bugs. Crash reports contain stack traces, device model, OS version, and app version. They do not contain your financial data. You can opt out in Settings → Data & Privacy.

10. Crowd Benchmarks and Merchant Intelligence

To make the app smarter for everyone, PocketBudJet contributes a small amount of anonymized data to a shared pool hosted by JosspaTech (PocketBase, plus the JosspaTech Cloudflare Worker key-value store). You can turn this off any time in Settings → Data & Privacy → "Help PBJ learn from everyone." Turning it off stops outbound contributions; you still receive the benefit of other users' contributions in your category suggestions.

What is contributed:

  • Merchant → category mappings (e.g. "Target → Groceries"). One vote per confirmation. No amounts, dates, or identifiers. Categories must be one of PocketBudJet's default category groups — generic merchant-name buckets (e.g. "Amazon", "Walmart") are filtered out and never become votes.
  • Suggested category splits (added May 2026): when you accept or adjust a suggested category split on a multi-category merchant (e.g. Walmart trip = 60% Groceries / 30% Household / 10% Clothing), the merchant slug, a coarse amount bucket (e.g. "$100–$300"), and the percentage distribution are sent. The actual transaction amount is never sent — only the bucket it falls into. No dates, no user ID, no device ID. This powers the credibility-blended split suggestions you see for big-box merchants.
  • Few-shot extraction examples (added May 2026): when you confirm a forwarded email transaction, the line items (e.g. "eggs, bread, Tide pods") and the category you confirmed are stored on the Worker as an example for that merchant, capped at 5 most-recent per merchant. The Worker uses them to prompt Anthropic on future emails. See §4 for detail.
  • Bucketed spending benchmarks by category, keyed to zip code and country, using coarse buckets so individual transactions are never reconstructable. No user ID, device ID, or IP.
  • Product intelligence: if you scan a barcode, the UPC is queried against public databases (upcitemdb.com, openfoodfacts.org). The UPC is all that's sent.

What is NEVER contributed to the crowd pool: your actual transaction amounts, transaction dates, account numbers, merchant strings the model can't confidently slugify, your name, your email, your phone, your device identifier, your IP address, your zip code outside of the §10 benchmark feature (which itself is opt-in), or any link back to you.

This pool powers features like "how does my Groceries spend compare to others in my zip?", auto-categorization suggestions on imports, and credibility-blended split suggestions on big-box merchants. JosspaTech may use the aggregated merchant database for commercial purposes, including licensing to third parties. Individual user data is not identifiable in the pool.

11. Other Network Services

  • Exchange rates (open.er-api.com): only currency pair codes (e.g. "USD→EUR") are sent. No amounts, no user data.
  • PC dashboard (optional): a local-only web server on your home network. Data stays inside your LAN.

12. Children's Privacy

PocketBudJet is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us at support@josspatech.com.

13. Data Deletion

Because your financial data is stored on your device (and any backups you made to a cloud provider you control), you delete it by uninstalling the app and, if applicable, deleting your backups from your cloud provider. JosspaTech does not retain a copy of your financial data and has nothing to delete on our end. If you've enabled Email Forwarding, disabling it (Settings → Import & Connections → Email Forwarding) removes your forwarding-address mapping from the Worker. Anonymous crash reports and aggregated crowd-pool contributions cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be individually withdrawn — but you can turn off future contributions at any time in Settings.

Not a diamond for its own sake - a solar system. Universe idea, simplified, with diamond bodies instead of orbs.

Story behind the mark

Started as the universe. That was too hard to draw simply. Became a solar system. Orbs felt generic, so planets became diamonds - stilized bodies on an orbit around a gold sun.

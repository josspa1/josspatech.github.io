JosspaTech logo system

Built from what iconic logos share - not from a template, and not locked to the old mark.

What iconic logos share

Nike, Apple, Target, Shell, Adidas - different industries, same underlying craft rules.

  • 1. One silhouette Drawable from memory. If you need a paragraph to describe it, it is too complex.
  • 2. One signature detail Apple's bite, FedEx's arrow, Target's center. One clever move - not twelve.
  • 3. Scales hard Still reads at favicon size. Ornament dies first; geometry survives.
  • 4. Works in mono Full color is a luxury. Black or white must still be the brand.
  • 5. Timeless geometry Avoid trend chrome. Clean shape outlives style cycles.

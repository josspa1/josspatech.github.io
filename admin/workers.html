Rough planning scenarios — assumes most users scan a few receipts or identify a few items per month, not hundreds per day. If the red banner or circuit breaker fires while you're still under ~50 subs, something is misconfigured or being abused — investigate before paying for API upgrades.

Founder economics — fixed costs, breakeven & when to upgrade APIs

PocketBase cron heartbeat — PBJ flywheel only (not urgent for HHH/CVC)

The Cron Health card below checks whether PocketBase's hourly aggregation task is running. That task drains merchant / paycheck / bill submission queues into the crowd library — it matters for Pocket Budget Journal's flywheel, not for Handy Horology or Curator's Vault identification.

Gray or missing heartbeat? The pb_hooks folder in the PBJ repo is not deployed to PocketHost yet — the heartbeat collection may not exist until hooks are uploaded. The dashboard shows this in the gray info banner at the top (not the red ops alert). HHH/CVC workers and identification are unaffected.

Red ops alert only fires for real outages (worker down), Gemini ≥80%, or an armed circuit breaker — not for missing pb_hooks.

Separate from this: the PBJ Cloudflare Worker's hourly cron sends quota alert emails (Gemini 80%, circuit breaker) — that runs on Workers, not PocketBase.

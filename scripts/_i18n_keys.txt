In plain English
In plain English Your information lives on your phone, and only on your phone. The app keeps it there so it can show it to you — and we never keep a copy of it anywhere, and we can't see it. There's no bank login, so the app never receives your bank password or account numbers. And there's no account and no sign-in, so nothing is tied to your name — to anyone but you, it's just numbers and transactions, with no name on them. When you show the app a statement, it reads it, does the math, and puts the results on your phone. That's the whole story.

## Production Notes

- **No brand logos.** Do not display, reference, or imply specific industry leaders by logo, name, or icon. Use neutral silhouettes or category-level swatches where the original concept used logos.
- **Data Presentation:** Clean, minimal charts. One stat per shot.
- **Color Coding:** Green for PocketBudJet advantages, red/grey for category-average gaps. Visual hierarchy should be obvious.
- **Font:** Bold, sans-serif. White on dark blue, high contrast for mobile.
- **Tone:** Confident but not arrogant. Factual, not emotional. Let the numbers speak.
- **Music:** Upbeat, modern. Slightly more energetic than the privacy-pitch video.
- **Pacing:** Quick cuts (2–3 sec per comparison). Keep momentum high.
- **Josspa:** Optional. If used, a confident nod or pointing gesture works well here.
- **All comparison facts must be verifiable from public listings as of the recording date.** No internal-research-only claims. The category-average ranges quoted are derived from public pricing pages.

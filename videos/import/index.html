Slide 1

Download from your bank

Log into your bank's website or app. Tap Download Transactions, Export Activity, or Statements. Choose CSV when available. Save to your phone — or skip the save step and Share → PocketBudJet directly from the bank app.

Slide 2

Share from bank app, or pick a file format

Fastest: in your bank app, open the statement and tap Share → PocketBudJet — skips your Downloads folder entirely. Or export a file: choose CSV when available (Smart Mapper handles it best), else OFX or QFX, then import from file or the Import folder. Full share walkthrough: Share bank statements.

Slide 3

Grab at least three months

In the date-range picker, select at least three months of history — six months or a year is better. Tap Download or Export. More months means smarter AI and sharper trend reports.

Slide 4

Tap Share from your bank app

Open PocketBudJet → Transactions → Import Center. Tap Share from your bank app (FASTEST). In your bank app, open the statement and tap Share → PocketBudJet. Or tap Or browse files to pick CSV/OFX/PDF, or save to the Import folder and tap the green Import banner on Home.

Slide 5

Tap Confirm when rows look right

The Smart Mapper reads your bank format automatically. Scan the draft list — green rows are confident; flagged rows need a quick look. Tap Confirm at the bottom when you are ready.

Slide 6

Tap Receipt Scanner to snap a receipt

No bank file? Tap + on Transactions, choose Receipt Scanner, and point at any receipt. OCR reads merchant, amount, date, and line items — printed or handwritten.

Slide 7

Tap WiFi ADF Scanner, feed the stack

Tap WiFi ADF Scanner on the Smart Scanning screen. Connect your home document feeder, load bank statements or receipts, and walk away — every page gets read and categorized.

Slide 8

Drop your file in the Import zone

Pair the PC Web Dashboard over local WiFi (Premium, included in the 21-day trial). Download your bank CSV on the computer, then drag it into the Import drop zone — same parsing as on your phone.

Slide 9

Tap Settings → Export for six formats

Tap the gear icon (top-right) → Export. Premium includes CSV, JSON, PDF, Excel, Word, OFX, and HTML. Exports land in your PocketBudJet Export folder for 30 days.

Done

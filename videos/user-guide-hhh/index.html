Transcript

Android: Open the Google Play internal testing link from josspatech.com/#hhh, opt in with your Google account, then tap Install.

IOS: Email support@josspatech.com for a TestFlight invite. Install TestFlight, accept the invite, then install Handy Horology Helper.

Open HHH after install. Swipe the welcome slides — identify pieces, track your collection, hunt on eBay. Tap Continue on each slide.

On the last welcome slide, tap Get Started. You land on How do you want to start?

Tap Explore with sample collection to tour Harold's demo gallery — recommended for first launch.

Or tap Start with my own piece to add your first watch from camera or manual entry.

If you picked sample collection, wait while Harold's demo watches load, then tap Get Started on the confirmation screen.

When sample data is active, notice the banner reminding you these are demo pieces.

Tap Clear sample data in Settings when you are ready for your own museum.

You land on Home — the Command Center. The status line shows piece count and wish-list size.

Use the quick command row: Hunt opens your wish list, Fix clock starts Clock Repair Help, Add Watch opens My Museum, Tools opens the toolkit grid.

Scroll the getting started cards to jump straight to Hunt on eBay, troubleshoot a clock, start your museum, or identify a piece.

The bottom tab bar has five tabs: Home, My Pieces, Tools, Collectors, and Settings. Tap any tab to switch.

Tap the My Pieces tab — second icon from the left — to open My Museum.

Switch segments at the top: Owned shows your collection, Wish holds grail targets, For Sale lists pieces you are selling.

The hero card shows total estimated value, gain or loss line, and a sparkline of collection value over time.

Tap the search field and type a brand or model. The list filters as you type.

Tap any row to open Piece Detail — photos, provenance, service history, and estimated value.

On Piece Detail, swipe through photos. Brand, model, reference, and estimated value sit below the gallery.

Scroll to Provenance to record where you bought the piece, serial numbers, and collector notes.

Tap Repairs and Service to log cleanings, repairs, and costs — each entry updates your cost basis.

Tap the Wish segment to see grail targets linked to Grail Radar hunt rules.

Tap For Sale to list pieces you are actively selling with asking price and notes.

On My Pieces, tap More for Timeline, Finances, Insurance Report, Service Tracker, and eBay match alerts.

On My Pieces, tap the gold Add button on the action bar — center of the bottom bar.

Tap Manual to enter details yourself instead of running AI Identify.

Fill brand, model, reference, serial, purchase price, and optional notes.

Tap Save. HHH adds the piece to your Owned collection immediately.

To add with AI, tap Identify on the My Museum action bar — or open Tools and tap Identify.

Tap Take Photo. Frame the dial straight-on with even light — one clear photo is enough to start.

Or tap Choose Photo to pick from your gallery — useful for photos from a show or estate sale.

When prompted, tap Add movement photo for better accuracy — or tap Skip Identify now to proceed with dial only.

Scroll to What do you know? Enter brand guess, model, reference, serial, or free-text clues.

Tap Wristwatch, Pocket Watch, or Clock if the auto-detected type is wrong.

Tap Identify this timepiece. Wait while HHH reads your photo and searches reference databases.

On the results screen, read the confidence percentage on the top match. Tap Other possibilities to compare alternatives.

If the top match looks right, tap This is correct. If not, tap a different possibility from the list below.

When confidence is low, tap Add detail photos for bezel text, logos, or serial close-ups — then tap Identify again.

Tap What I know to correct brand or model before re-running. Your edits override the AI guess on the next pass.

Tap Save to Collection. HHH creates a museum entry with photos, brand, model, and estimated value pre-filled.

After saving a clock, tap Find parts on Clockworks to jump to movement-aware parts search from this identification.

With no connection, HHH queues your identification and runs it when you are back online — check the banner on Home.

Tap Fix clock on the Home quick commands row. The Clock Repair Help wizard opens.

Or tap Tools, scroll to Clock Repair Help, and tap the card.

Pick the symptom that matches: Won't chime, Pendulum stops, Hands slip, or another option from the list.

Review the plain-language repair steps HHH suggests for your symptom. Follow safety notes before opening the case.

Scroll to Suggested parts on Clockworks. SKUs and product names appear when the catalog is connected.

Tap a product row or Shop on Clockworks to open the item in your browser.

Complete checkout on Clockworks.com — HHH does not process payments for parts.

Tap Hunt on Home — or open My Pieces and switch to the Wish segment — to see grail targets.

Tap the plus button on Wish list. Enter brand, model, and a target price you are willing to pay.

Tap a wish-list row to open Grail Radar for that grail. Hunt rules and last-check time appear at the top.

Edit keywords and max price under Hunt rules. Tap Save hunt rules when done.

Tap Check now to search eBay immediately. Matches appear with listing title, price, and link.

Tap any match to open the eBay listing in your browser.

When a new match arrives, HHH sends a notification. Tap it to jump straight to that grail's Grail Radar screen.

From Tools, open eBay Listings to search sold and active listings by brand and model.

Enter brand and model, then review active and sold comps before you commit to a purchase.

On My Pieces, tap More, then Finances — or Gains and Losses — for portfolio-level profit and loss.

The dashboard shows total cost basis, estimated value, and net gain or loss across all owned pieces.

On any Piece Detail screen, tap Edit and enter Purchase price. HHH recalculates gain or loss against estimated value.

Under Repairs and Service, tap Add entry. Enter date, vendor, and cost — service spend adds to your cost basis.

Tap More, then Insurance Report. HHH generates a PDF-ready summary of pieces, values, and photos for your insurer.

From Finances or the More menu, open Budget Tracker.

Set a monthly or annual acquisition budget and track spend against it as you add pieces.

From Tools, scroll to Web Companion and tap the card. HHH starts a local server on your phone.

On your PC browser — same Wi-Fi as the phone — scan the QR code shown on screen.

On the PC dashboard, browse pieces, add watches, and run Grail Hunt — changes sync back to the phone over LAN.

Tap Settings, scroll to Backup and Restore — or open Backup from Tools — to manage your .hhh backup file.

Tap Export backup. Choose Drive, iCloud, email, or Files on the share sheet.

On a new phone, tap Restore backup and pick your .hhh file. Restore replaces all local museum data — confirm before proceeding.

Pro subscribers can enable optional cloud sync in Backup settings. Your collection stays on your device by default.

Tap the Settings tab — gear icon at bottom right.

Tap Theme and switch Light or Dark. The app updates immediately.

Tap Language. HHH supports seven display languages — pick one and confirm.

Under Security, enable App lock for PIN or biometrics when opening HHH.

Under Notifications, allow eBay Grail Radar alerts and service reminders.

HHH includes a 14-day Pro trial with full access — no card required on Android internal testing.

After the trial, tap Upgrade in Settings. Choose $9.99 per month or $74.99 per year.

Manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play or App Store — Settings, Subscription, Manage.

Tap the Tools tab — center icon — to open the horology toolkit grid.

Tap What's It Worth for AI price guidance on a piece — Pro feature.

Tap Compare. Pick two pieces from your museum to see side-by-side specs, value, and photos.

Tap Condition Assessment. Answer checklist questions about case, dial, and movement condition.

Tap Collection Value to see how your portfolio estimated value changed over time.

Tap Trade Analyzer to model a trade — your piece plus cash versus theirs — Pro feature.

Tap Ask the Expert for AI chat about horology questions — Pro feature.

Tap Take Photos for guided step-by-step shots — dial, caseback, movement — Pro feature.

Tap Digital ID Card to generate a proof-of-ownership card with photos and key details — Pro feature.

Tap Scan Barcode. Point the camera at a UPC or QR on packaging or paperwork.

Tap Scan Papers to photograph receipts, certificates, or appraisal documents and attach them to a piece.

Tap How Complex Is It. Select complications to rate movement complexity.

Tap Is It Accurate. Log time checks against a reference and track daily rate over weeks.

Tap What to Wear — Rotation Planner. Pick pieces for the week so nothing sits unworn too long.

Tap Warranties. Add expiry dates — HHH reminds you before they lapse.

Tap Shows and Events. Add watch fairs, club meetings, and auction dates to your calendar.

Tap Exact Time. HHH shows atomic-clock reference time — use it to set your watch or verify rate.

Tap Moon Phase. See tonight's moon phase and set your moonphase complication dial.

Tap Print and Export List. Generate a PDF table of your collection, wish list, or for-sale pieces.

Tap Nearby Finds for flea-market and estate-sale alerts near you — Pro feature.

Tap Share Nearby to send a piece summary to another phone over Bluetooth — Pro feature.

Tap View on Big Screen for a LAN slideshow of your collection on a TV or projector.

From Tools, open Movement to Parts to search the Clockworks catalog from a movement photo or identification.

Questions? Email support@josspatech.com or use in-app feedback.
